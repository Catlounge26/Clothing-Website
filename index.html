Why the trims aren’t natural

The only part that isn’t natural fibre is the thin elastic in the waistband and leg edging. Cotton on its own doesn’t spring back, so a small amount of stretch fibre is what keeps the shape, wash after wash.

We keep it to the edges only. The body and the lining stay natural.

Why this shop exists

A few quality options that don’t take your time

“I want to offer a place where you get exactly what you came to find, and leave with that.”

I got tired with the fact that I would spend hours trying to find natural fibre underwear that would also look good and be comfortable. It was almost impossible. I don’t want more options, I want a few quality options that don’t take my time.

Every product we offer is made of 100% or at least 80% natural fibre. The underwear lining is always natural fibre. The trims are the only part where we need to use another fibre to keep the shape. Each underwear is made to be comfortable. No seams at the bottom, no plasticky straps. We also offer 100% natural fibre underwear for the comfy girl.

I always take longer to purchase if I have too many options, and just a simple buy becomes a burdening task instead of fun and simple. So I built this site to offer all the things I always found so difficult to find, but that always were the staple pieces.
